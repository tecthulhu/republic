# DEC-0009 — The consolidation: five acceptance sets, SPEC-0086's control, the artifacts-marker rule

and Duo-approved under DEC-0008. Signed by the owner's merge (PA-002, ENT-079).

```

```

Verified against `.ferry/enrolled_machines` as the trust anchor rather than against the
signature's own embedded key, which would have been circular. The register row is the
certificate, exactly as DEC-0008 ratified, and it did the job on its first use.

**The authorization half does not verify here, and that is a finding rather than a
complaint.** The manifest's signed body carries `AUTH kyle-scott 2026-08-20 AUTHORIZED`
and no transaction reference. The Duo txid arrived in the covering message, out of
band. So reception can establish *which machine shipped this* cryptographically, and
can establish only that *the machine asserts a human approved* — the witnessed record
that would join the two is not in anything the receiving plane holds.

The charter's own §6 specifies the custody line as *"manifest `<digest>` — approved by
`<principal>` (duo txid `<id>`, digest-in-push) — shipped from `<label>`"*. Two thirds
of that line is verifiable today. The fix is small and belongs in the manifest schema:
put the txid inside the signed body, where the machine signature binds it. A txid
supplied alongside the shipment is an assertion about evidence; a txid inside the
signed manifest is evidence — and that distinction is the whole subject of the
principal-verifier contract's §1.3.

## Act 1 — five acceptance sets ratified

Every criterion across STORY-0013/0014/0015/0016/0017 moves `proposed → ratified`,
each carrying `authorized_by: DEC-0009`. Serialized once instead of five times, per
DEC-0005's rule that ratification is a signed act rather than a merge side-effect.

**Their binding rules ratify in the same act, and that is load-bearing.** RULE-0082–0088,
0094 and 0095 were all `proposed`. Ratifying nine claims while their rules stayed
proposed would have left nine ratified-but-unbound claims and driven the coverage meter
from 13 to 22 — the opposite of this ceremony's purpose, achieved by doing exactly what
it said. The instruction named the criteria; the arithmetic named the rules.

## Act 2 — SPEC-0086 binds

CTRL-0011 is authored and RULE-0100 binds `SPEC-0086 → CTRL-0011 → ENF-0001`. This was
the deliberate unbound claim: DEC-0005 declined to bind it to a control that did not
exist and carried it openly as a meter line for six days rather than hiding it.

`in_force: true` — the check runs in this PR, is wired into the corpus job, and passes
against the real Acta: 855 pin-bearing rows, one declared version, one binary hash.
Six fixtures prove it can fail.

## Act 3 — the artifacts-marker rule

SPEC-0131 → v1.4.0. In `artifacts/` the folder declares the type and a marker is
optional. Nine digest-pinned deliverables go green **with zero bytes changed** — all
nine still verify against their original receipt.

## Deltas from the predicted meters

Three of four predictions hold exactly. The fourth needed more than the amendment named:

**The ferry's own shipment tripped the lint it shipped through.** Landing
`SHIP-20260820T203311` put four files in the tree that the ingest rules could not
classify: the two trust registers under `.ferry/`, the manifest's `<!-- ingest: manifest -->`
type, and the detached `.sig`. So v1.4.0 also exempts dot-prefixed infrastructure paths
and `.sig` control files, and declares `manifest` as a type — which the charter already
required when it told the sweeper to look for an "ingest-lint-conformant shipment
manifest". A vocabulary that had no word for the document the mechanism produces was
going to fail on first contact, and did.

## What ratifying moves

- Nineteen atoms `proposed → ratified`; the reconcile then activates the nine claims,
  since each is bound by a rule ratified in the same act, and activates CTRL-0011,
  whose implementation resolves.
- `unbound_claims` **13 → 12**: SPEC-0086 binds and leaves. SPEC-0135 stays, honestly —
  it is `check: human` and its control is a reader.
- The five stories themselves stay `proposed`. The instruction ratifies criteria, not
  stories, and that is right: a criterion is the standard, and a story ratifies when its
  work is done and evidenced. STORY-0016 is not built.
