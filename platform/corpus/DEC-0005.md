# DEC-0005 — The walked hop, ratified: the first floor touch of acceptance criteria

Authored against `corpus@3aaf494bed050efa` and signed by the owner's merge
(PA-002, ENT-079). Floor direction:
`BRIDGE_ratifying_DEC_and_STORY0014.md`, Direction 1.

**What this decision is for.** STORY-0002's acceptance set has been green, merged
and walked since PR #24. Its six criteria read `proposed / authorized_by: null`
the whole time, and so did the story. The floor's queue expected the reconcile
pass to move them; it cannot — `enact.py --reconcile` computes ONT-060's
`ratified → active` trigger, and `proposed → ratified` has no computed trigger by
design, because ratification is a signed act (D34). Nothing was going to move
those atoms until someone decided to.

**Why that is worth a paragraph rather than a line.** No acceptance specification
in this corpus has ever been ratified. Every SPEC- in every story's acceptance set
has sat at `proposed` from the day it was written, which means the platform has
been grading its own work against criteria that no human ever put a signature
under — not because anyone waived the requirement, but because the mechanism for
meeting it had never been exercised and its absence looked like every other
`proposed` atom. **This decision is the first floor touch of acceptance criteria in
the corpus's history**, and it is the anchor STORY-0014's baseline pin needs: until
a floor touch exists, "grade against the last floor-touched instance" resolves to
nothing and a pin built on it would report green while pinning nothing.

**Bindings, and one deliberate omission.** SPEC-0081 through SPEC-0085 are bound to
CTRL-0005 by RULE-0089–0093, ratified in the same act, so ONT-031 is satisfied and
the reconcile that follows can activate them. SPEC-0086 is ratified and left
unbound: the CLI pin rides every evidence row this platform emits and no control
asserts it, so a rule binding it to CTRL-0005 would be a rule with no enforcing
check — SPEC-0091's shape, which this corpus has already been caught by twice.
It becomes one visible `unbound_claims` line rather than a hidden hole, and closes
when its control lands (floor Finding 4, STORY-0014's window).

**Effects and activation are separate.** This decision ratifies. The reconcile that
follows activates whatever ONT-060 finds eligible, attributed to the law rather
than to this signature (D34) — a signature must never read as having moved the
ontology's lifecycle.

## What the meters will read afterwards

Recorded before the ceremony runs, so the numbers can be checked against the claim
rather than read off the result.

- `unbound_claims` moves from 11 to **12**: SPEC-0086 joins, and the five bound
  criteria do not.
- SPEC-0081–0085 and STORY-0002 activate on the reconcile; SPEC-0086 stays
  `ratified`.
- No claim is retired and no atom moves backwards. ONT-060 has no backwards edge
  and a ceremony must not invent one.

## What this decision deliberately does not do

It does not ratify STORY-0013's acceptance set (SPEC-0122–0125), which is equally
green and equally untouched. The floor directed this one, and a ceremony that
quietly widens its own scope is the shape the whole program guards against — an
agent deciding what counts as ratified is precisely the authority SPEC-0123 says
agents do not have. STORY-0013's set awaits its own floor direction.
