## This decision is the floor touch STORY-0016 proved was required

The posture's own text said it carried a retirement date. The ratified instance carried
none, and STORY-0016 established by blocked attempt that no agent could supply one:

- the in-place edit was refused by SPEC-0113 — a published instance edited in place;
- the lawful new instance was flagged by SPEC-0127 — an untouched change to the
  criteria of an in-flight story, with SPEC-0126 continuing to grade against the pin;
- and the schema refused `authorized_by: null` on an active atom, closing the last
  route.

Three independent mechanisms, none written for this case, converging on the same
refusal. **The doors that refused the agent open to the floor's signed act**, and that
asymmetry is the entire content of the mitigation: not that criteria cannot move, but
that they move only under a touch an agent cannot perform.

So this ceremony does the thing the machinery reserved for it. `retirement_date:
2026-10-20`, sixty days from the ruling.

## The time-box is enforced, not decorative

CTRL-0005's honestly-skipped sub-claim becomes checked. The check asserts the date is
present, and **fails the tree once the date passes** while the posture is still
standing. Expiry is a failure rather than a warning, because the posture's own text says
it must not silently persist — and a check that merely noted the lapse would be exactly
the promise-to-revisit PRIN-0005 rules against. Verified in both directions: green at
sixty days out, red when the date is moved into the past.

On 2026-10-20 this goes red unless the floor re-ratifies, supersedes, or retires it.
That is the intended behaviour, and it is worth being plain that the red is a feature
someone will have to answer in October.

with reception able to verify all three thirds of the custody line. It is not, and
reception cannot. The manifest's signed body carries `AUTH kyle-scott 2026-08-21

Recorded because the document would otherwise stand as law asserting a property its
