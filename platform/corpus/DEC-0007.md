# DEC-0007 — Law injection: the knowledge plane and the enforcement plane

Enrolled from `CEREMONY_DEC0006_enact_and_DEC0007_injection_corrected.md`. ID minted at
enrollment per the ID-at-enrollment discipline; the author does not mint.

Signed by the owner's merge (PA-002, ENT-079). The prior draft carried a written
signature block; that path is **struck** — two mechanisms for one act is the
two-sources shape, and DEC-0006 settled that the merge is the signature.

## The question, and why it splits in two

`injection_set` selected law by `state == "active"`. Two planes were collapsed inside
that one filter:

- The **knowledge plane** asks *what law does a citizen know?* Its correctness
  condition is completeness of ratified law.
- The **enforcement plane** asks *what law is armed around a citizen?* Its correctness
  condition is honest reporting, not completeness — binding work legitimately lags
  ratification.

Collapsed, they produce one of two standing defects: citizens ignorant of law that
binds them, or gates asserted as armed that are not. This decision separates them.

## What measurement changed in the draft, and why it matters

Two of the prior draft's five obligations were unsatisfiable, and both failures were
the same failure: a disposition written from what the corpus was assumed to contain
rather than from what it does.

**O1** required "6 of 6 ratified documents present". There are **zero** ratified
documents — the four missing ones are `draft`. The filter change is therefore a
**no-op for documents** and moves only restrictions, 12 to 16. It is kept because it
is right in principle and will matter the moment a document is ratified; it is not
credited with closing the gap that motivated it.

**O2** would have marked the twelve active restrictions `in_force: true`. Nothing in
this platform evaluates a restriction around a session — `armed_restrictions` is read
by no runtime code, and `spawn.py` claimed a pre/post evaluation that does not exist.
Marking them enforced would have committed the precise defect O2 was written to
prevent, inside the disposition preventing it.

The general lesson is worth keeping: **a ruling written against a described system
inherits that description's errors.** Both were caught by measuring before
implementing, which is the only reason this decision is enactable as written.
