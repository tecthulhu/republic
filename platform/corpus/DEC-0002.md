## Preconditions (both required before the owner signs)

1. **Ownership memo.** A one-page memo, reviewed by counsel, recording
   that republic_semantic_mesh (and the senate / senate-forum substrates
   it composes with) are Kyle Scott's personal works held in the
   personally-owned tecthulhu org; that they are a separate expression
   from the Cornerstone platform owned by ABG; and that the boundary is
   the standing scope law (adapts-to-infrastructure vs.
   provides-infrastructure). If counsel instead concludes any component
   belongs on ABG's ledger, this decision is superseded by a variant in
   which an assignment precedes the grant — the license text is
   unchanged, only the grantor line moves.
2. **Counsel pass on the license choice itself** against the ruled
   criteria: prevent hosted forks; permit internal corporate use with
   license and notices intact; keep the OSI open-source label.

## Enactment mechanics

The signing act is the owner's commit adding the verbatim AGPL-3.0 text
as `LICENSE` at repo root plus SPDX headers policy (headers required in
source files; governed corpus documents are covered by the repository
license and carry no per-file headers). `process_ref` is set to that
commit's hash; the commit message names the org/repo at time of signing.
Contributor terms for any future external contribution (inbound=outbound
vs. DCO vs. CLA) are explicitly out of scope here and reserved for a
future decision before the first external PR is accepted.
