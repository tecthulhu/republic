# DEC-0006 — Instructions to the mesh are governed artifacts

Authored from `DEC_instructions_are_governed.md`, item 2 of the ingest bootstrap,
consumed through `atomic_ingest/` per the discipline it establishes. Signed by the
owner's merge (PA-002, ENT-079).

**Proposed, and correctly so until the floor ratifies.** The source document is
explicit that this decision must not activate before its enforcing check exists. The
check now exists (CTRL-0010, SPEC-0131), so the dependency is satisfied and the
decision is ratifiable — but ratification is the floor's act, not the author's, which
is the whole content of the thing being decided.

## The self-consistency note, for the record

This decision — that instructions are governed — entered as a governed instruction: a
document placed in `atomic_ingest/proposed/`, consumed through the sanctioned channel,
authored here as a proposed atom, ratified by the floor. The rule that instructions are
governed is installed by a governed instruction, and the only ungoverned act in the
sequence is the initiator that delivered the queue.

That is the same move DEC-0001 made, when a hand-edit installed the machinery that
ended hand-edits. A bootstrap has to stand somewhere, and the honest version marks the
spot rather than pretending the ground was always there.

## One thing the source document asked for that this decision does not do

The enforcement clause reads that the decision "is enforced by the ingest lint". It is
not, yet — not in the sense the word carries everywhere else in this corpus.
`atomic_ingest/` lives outside the repository, so no gate runs CTRL-0010 and no merge
is refused on its verdict. CTRL-0010 is a control; enforcement is absent.

The distinction is exactly the one STORY-0017 was written to make visible one layer
down, and it would have been easy to let it collapse here, in a decision about
governance, one day after building the control that exists to keep it from collapsing.
So the decision below binds to the lint as its control and names the enforcement gap in
its own outcome. The binding rules carry `ENF-0004` (advisory), not `ENF-0001` (block
merge), because a rule may not claim to stop a merge that nothing stops.

## Why the whole bootstrap rides one decision

Items 1–5 are separable as work and inseparable as law. The posture (SPEC-0132) is
empty without the channel the decision establishes; the decision's outcome is untrue
without the lint (SPEC-0131) behind it; the state check (SPEC-0133) is an extension of
that lint; and the plan (STRAT-0002) exists to say when all three retire. Ratifying
them apart would leave the corpus briefly asserting a channel with no check, or a check
governing a channel no decision established.
